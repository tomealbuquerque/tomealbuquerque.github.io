05 / ABOUT

A bridge between disciplines.

I'm a machine learning scientist working at the intersection of representation learning, biological imaging and drug discovery. My background spans biochemistry, biomedical engineering and AI.

Experience
August 2024 – Present

Johnson & Johnson

Post Doctoral Researcher - AI/ML in Drug Discovery, Zug, Switzerland

  • Led development and deployment of scalable ML infrastructure for high-content imaging, enabling large-scale ViT-based featurization across millions of images and reducing end-to-end computation time by 10x.
  • Developed and benchmarked foundation models for cellular imaging at 100+ TB scale (JUMP-CP), spanning representation learning, morphological profiling and large-scale biological evaluation.
  • Built multimodal learning systems connecting cellular imaging, transcriptomics and molecular structure, alongside reusable ML pipelines for pharmaceutical research.
December 2023 – May 2024

Roche

PhD Intern - Digital Pathology Product R&D, Basel, Switzerland

  • Developed an ABMIL interpretability framework to detect spurious correlations in diagnostic pathology models using tile- and WSI-level confounder injection and quantitative localization metrics.
  • Integrated advanced XAI tooling into production pathology pipelines through close collaboration with R&D engineers, accelerating deployment of reliable and interpretable model outputs.
October 2019 – March 2024

VCMI, INESC TEC

Research Assistant / PhD Student, Porto, Portugal

  • Developed Clinical Decision Support Systems (DSS) for cervical cancer screening using computer vision and ordinal inference.
  • Designed deep learning models for pathological image quality assessment in partnership with Leica Biosystems and IMP.
  • Built XAI systems (TAMI Project) with Carnegie Mellon University and engineered EDoF computational microscopy methods with Fraunhofer Portugal AICOS for resource-limited screening settings.
October 2022 – March 2023

Technical University of Munich

Guest PhD Student, Schuffler Lab, Munich, Germany

  • Developed multimodal deep learning architectures for identification of brain tumor biomarkers (IDH1 and 1p19q) by fusing MRI and whole-slide images (digital pathology) using data-fusion and context-aware strategies.
September 2018 – July 2019

FEUP / INEGI

Graduate Researcher, Porto, Portugal

  • Conducted computational biomechanics research on peripheral nerve tissue regeneration using finite element method (FEM) and meshless methods for structural simulation.
